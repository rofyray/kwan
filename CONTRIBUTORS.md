## How names get added

A maintainer adds your name in the same pull request that brings in your work, or in the pull request that imports your review sheet. You choose how your name is shown, and you can ask for it to be changed or removed at any time. Teacher reviewers and translators may also list the region where they teach and the languages they reviewed. We never list an email address or a phone number.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for how to take part.
